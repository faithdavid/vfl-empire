How to use

  • Bet slate sum: use Angle 1 + 4 (MD bias + Markov from prev slate).
  • Bet single fixture: combine Angle 2–3 with table cell (prior script) + same pairing.
  • X-2: filter fixtures where prev2_md_sum_odd agrees with prev_md_sum_odd (both even or both odd) — narrows sample, may lift %.
